# Ambience recordings

openOMSI's ambience is synthesised (wind, gusts, leaves, rain drops, thunder, crickets, the
town's hum, the tyres on every road surface - `crates/omsi-audio/src/ambient`), except for
the birdsong below. Every file here is in the public domain or under CC0 1.0, so it may be
used and redistributed freely; the authors are named as a courtesy.

Each was downloaded from its page's official download link and converted for openOMSI: a
180 Hz high-pass (against wind and traffic rumble), loudness normalisation (EBU R128 to
-24 LUFS, true peak under -3 dBFS), mono, 32 kHz, FLAC. `birds_morning_flock.flac` is the
first 120 s of its source.
